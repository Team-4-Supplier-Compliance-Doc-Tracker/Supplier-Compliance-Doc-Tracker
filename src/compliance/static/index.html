Know which supplier documents need attention

Certificates expire unnoticed in email and spreadsheets. This tool checks your list, flags the problems, shows which parts are affected, and keeps a dated history for audits. It never approves or blocks anything: you decide.

How it works

  1. Read the list

    Takes the documents and parts on file, and rejects broken records with a reason.

  2. Flag the problems

    Finds what is expired, expiring or missing, and which parts that puts at risk.

  3. You decide

    Chase the supplier, then save the check so you can show what you knew on that date.

What the words mean
Expired
The expiry date has passed. A document is still valid on its expiry date.
Expiring soon
Valid now, but it runs out within the warning window of 45 days. This is a warning, so parts are not at risk yet.
Missing
A part needs this document type and the supplier has no usable one on file.
Waiting for approval
Received but not approved yet, so it does not count as on file.
At risk
A part whose own category needs a document that is expired or missing. Other parts from that supplier are not affected.
Could not use
A broken record, such as a bad date. It is rejected with a reason and never saved.
